## Hard logistics constraints discovered (bake into app + bookings)

1. **May 24 SIN→HKG flight must depart ~12:00 PM or later** — DCL guidance: no flights before 11:15 AM on debark day.
2. **China entry**: HK→Shanghai→Tokyo qualifies for the **240-hour visa-free transit** (HK counts as a distinct region; onward = Japan). No visa needed under current rules. TOP re-verify item ~Feb 2027; carry policy printout + Tokyo itinerary at HKG check-in. TWOV immigration lane can take 30–60 min.
3. **Vesak Day (Singapore public holiday) = Thu May 20, 2027** — embarkation day. Minimal impact; PH hours at breakfast spots.
4. **Great Firewall**: install a roaming travel eSIM (Airalo/Nomad/etc.) BEFORE landing in Shanghai — bypasses blocking without VPN. Set up Alipay (linked cards + passport verification) before arrival.
5. **Tokyo Disney free Priority Pass ends Aug 31, 2026** → assume paid Premier Access only in May 2027 (~¥16–24k family budget across 3 days). Space Mountain almost certainly still closed (2027 rebuild); Buzz Lightyear closed.
6. **Shinkansen oversized baggage**: bags >160cm linear need the (free, limited) oversized-baggage seat reservation, or use takkyubin luggage forwarding Tokyo→Kyoto (~¥2,000–2,500/bag, next-day; drop by Jun 3 morning).
7. **Date line**: depart KIX ~9 PM June 7 → land HNL ~9:30 AM the same morning, June 7. Kyoto June 7 is a ¾ day (Fushimi Inari early, wagashi class, Haruka ~4:45 PM).
8. **Dragon Boat Festival 2027 = June 9** — clears both the HK and Shanghai windows; no China holiday crowd surges expected (confirm 2027 calendar when published ~Dec 2026).
9. **Symphony of Lights (HK)** slated for retirement H2 2026 — don't promise the 8 PM show; skyline view stands on its own.
10. Weather arc: equatorial heat + afternoon storms (SIN/cruise/HK) → warm humid (Shanghai) → mild pre-tsuyu (Tokyo) → rainy-season onset risk in Kyoto (~Jun 6–7) → dry perfect Oahu. Ponchos + quick-dry everywhere; indoor backups researched per city.
