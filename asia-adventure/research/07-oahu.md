## STRUCTURE VERDICTS

**Recommended shape (2 nights, arrive ~9–10 AM Jun 7):**
- **Day 1 (Jun 7, arrival day):** Land mid-morning, drop bags in Waikiki (rooms likely not ready — beach bag packed on top). Easy beach/pool afternoon, shave ice, early casual dinner, crash early. No scheduled anything.
- **Day 2 (Jun 8, only full day):** Jet-lagged early wake → **Diamond Head sunrise hike** (gate 6 AM, 1.5–2 hr round trip, kid-easy). Back for breakfast, pool/beach mid-day, **Waikiki Aquarium** as the low-effort afternoon anchor, sunset dinner at Duke's.
- **Day 3 (Jun 9):** Beach morning if a red-eye or afternoon flight; head to HNL.

**Verdicts:**
- **Pearl Harbor: SKIP this trip.** It's a genuine half-day-plus (30–40 min drive each way, arrive 45–60 min early, 45-min program), tickets release 56 days ahead at 3 PM HST on Recreation.gov ($1/ticket, sell out fast; small next-day batch daily at 3 PM HST), and it's emotionally heavy — the opposite of decompression after 3 weeks of travel. Save it for a dedicated Oahu trip. (pearlharbor.org, recreation.gov, nps.gov)
- **Aulani day visit: SKIP.** Verified policy: **no day passes exist; pools, waterslides, lazy river, and beach gear are strictly for overnight guests** (daily-color wristband system, enforced). Non-guests can only eat at restaurants, shop, do the spa/luau, or use the public lagoon sand. 45–60 min drive from Waikiki for no pool access = not worth it. (plandisney.disney.go.com, daypassscout.com, disboards.com)
- **Waikiki Aquarium: YES — ideal gentle anchor.** $12 adults / $5 kids 4–12, open 9:00–4:30 daily, ~1 hr visit, walkable at the Diamond Head end of Waikiki next to Kapiolani Park. Jellyfish exhibit is the kid favorite; includes free mobile audio guide. (waikikiaquarium.org, Tripadvisor)
- **Diamond Head sunrise: YES.** Out-of-state visitors **must reserve timed entry in advance** — $5/person + $10 parking (skip parking; rideshare or walk from east Waikiki). 6:00 AM slot sells out in minutes; booking window opens at midnight HST (sources conflict: 14 vs 30 days ahead — verify). Late >30 min voids the reservation. (dlnr.hawaii.gov, hawaii-guide.com, honolulutravels.com)

## LOGISTICS

**Date-line math (get this right in the app):** The family leaves Kyoto **midday June 7** (Haruka express to KIX ~80 min), departs KIX on an **evening** flight (~8:45–10:10 PM JST is the standard KIX–HNL bank; ~7 hr flight), and lands in Honolulu **~9:00–10:00 AM the same morning, June 7** — clock-wise they "land before they took off." Hawaii is **19 hours behind Japan** (no DST in either). This is the day they *gain back* the one they lost flying to Asia. NOT a morning departure — evening departure, same-calendar-morning arrival. (flightsfrom.com, flyteam.jp)

**Jet lag:** Returning from Asia, the body runs early — expect 4–5 AM wakes for a couple of days. That's exactly why the Diamond Head sunrise slot and early Duke's seating work, and why late-night plans should not exist on this leg.

**HNL → Waikiki:** ~9 miles. Rideshare $25–45 (~25–35 min); Charley's Taxi flat-rate ~$29–35; Roberts Express Shuttle ~$18–20/person. With 4 people + 3 weeks of luggage: one XL rideshare or flat-rate taxi. Skyline rail doesn't usefully serve this trip. (hawaiitravelwithkids.com, kanoatransportation.com)

**Rental car verdict: NO CAR.** Everything on this itinerary (beach, aquarium, Duke's, garlic shrimp, Island Vintage) is walkable; Waiola and Diamond Head are cheap rideshares. Waikiki hotel parking runs $40–60+/night — two rideshare round trips beat that easily.

**HNL → PHX (Jun 9):** Nonstops on American, Southwest, and Hawaiian, ~5 hr 45 min–6 hr. Phoenix is **3 hours ahead** of Hawaii in June (AZ has no DST). Two viable shapes: morning departure (~10:20 AM → ~7:15 PM PHX, sane but eats the last day) or the **red-eye (~11:30 PM → ~8:15 AM PHX)**, which buys a full final beach day — with jet-lagged early risers, morning departure is probably kinder. (flightsfrom.com, airportoverview.com)

**June weather:** One of the driest, most reliable months — highs ~84–87°F, lows ~73–75°F, water ~78–79°F, brief passing trade-wind showers at most, ~11+ hrs of sun. Pack reef-safe sunscreen (Hawaii law bans oxybenzone/octinoxate sunscreens).
