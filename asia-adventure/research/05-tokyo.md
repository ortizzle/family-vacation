## LODGING VERDICT

**Recommendation: SPLIT STAY — 3 nights Maihama partner hotel (May 30–Jun 2) + 2 nights Tokyo Station/Marunouchi area (Jun 2–4).**

Why split wins here:
- Park days demand early starts (gates ~1 hr before open). Being 5–10 min from the gates vs 45–60 min from central Tokyo is the single biggest quality-of-life lever for 3 Disney days with kids.
- Ending at Tokyo Station area puts you (a) one direct 15-min Keiyo Line hop from Maihama for the move, (b) walkable to the June 4 Shinkansen platform with luggage, (c) central for city day 2.
- Cost: only one hotel move, mid-trip, on a park-to-city transition day (do it in the morning of Jun 2 OR use luggage forwarding/coin lockers).

## DISNEY 3-DAY PLAN

**Park split verdict: 2 days DisneySea + 1 day Disneyland — CONFIRMED for ages 13 & 10.**
- TDS is the consensus "best Disney park in the world," unique to Japan, physically huge (community's #1 mistake is underestimating its size/walking distances), and Fantasy Springs alone eats half a day. Ages 13/10 are ideal for its slightly older skew (real thrill rides + spectacle).
- TDL in May 2027 is at a construction low point: **Space Mountain demolished (closed Jul 2024; new ¥70.5B version "opens 2027" — almost certainly NOT by May; assume closed — FLAG verify)** and Buzz Lightyear closed (Wreck-It Ralph replacement not open). One TDL day covers its unique headliners comfortably on a low-crowd weekday.
- Suggested order: **TDL May 31 (Mon) → TDS Jun 1–2 (Tue–Wed)** — all weekdays, post-Golden-Week/pre-tsuyu trough.

**Fantasy Springs — rules have RELAXED (huge win):**
- Since 2025, **entry to the land is free/unrestricted** — no Standby Pass, no entry lottery. Walk in like any land; ride standby lines are normal queues.
- The 3 rides: **Anna & Elsa's Frozen Journey** (top priority, longest waits, sells out DPA first), **Rapunzel's Lantern Festival** (gorgeous boat ride, second priority), **Peter Pan's Never Land Adventure** (3D dark ride; motion-sensitive kids note, but 13/10 fine). Fairy Tinker Bell's Busy Buggies is a kiddie ride — skippable.
- DPA for Fantasy Springs rides: ¥2,000/person/ride. Strategy: rope-drop Frozen Journey standby, buy DPA for Rapunzel (or vice versa).

**Line-skip state (May 2027):**
- **Disney Premier Access (paid, app-only):** ¥1,500–2,000/ride/person; one purchase per ride per day. TDS lineup: Soaring, Journey to the Center of the Earth, Toy Story Mania, 3 Fantasy Springs rides, Believe! viewing. TDL lineup: Beauty and the Beast, Baymax, Splash Mountain + parade viewing. **Pooh's Hunny Hunt is NOT on DPA — rope-drop or single-digit-wait it on a low day.**
- **Priority Pass (free) is GONE after Aug 2026** — no announced replacement. FLAG: recheck early 2027; if a new free system exists, learn it before arrival.
- **Standby Pass** (free timed-entry virtual queue) may still gate some attractions/shops on busy days — check app on entry.
- Budget guidance: ~¥16,000–24,000 total (~$110–160) buys the family 2–3 DPAs across 3 days = transforms the trip. Worth it.

**Rope-drop reality:** Gates open 15–60 min before posted time (9:00 posted often = ~8:15–8:45 actual). Arrive **60+ min before posted open** at TDS, 45–60 at TDL. At open: one parent's phone buys DPA (party grouped in app), other parent leads the walk to first standby ride. TDS day 1: Frozen Journey or Soaring first. TDL: Pooh's Hunny Hunt or Beauty and the Beast first.

**Tickets — CRITICAL:**
- Date-specific, park-specific (no hopping until 6 PM w/ certain tickets). Sold ONLY online — **official app/website or Klook**; no gate sales. 1-day: roughly ¥7,900–10,900 adult, variable pricing.
- On sale **60 days ahead at 2:00 PM JST**. For May 31/Jun 1/Jun 2, 2027: windows open ~**Apr 1–3, 2027**. Weekday June dates rarely sell out post-anniversary, but buy at window-open anyway. Official app needed in-park regardless (DPA, mobile ordering, wait times) — set it up + create Disney account before leaving home.

**Crowds late May/early June:** verified LOW — rainy-season weekdays are the lightest of the year at TDS; 2027 loses the 25th-anniversary crowd inflation. Expect walk-ons for mid-tier rides, 45–90 min for Frozen Journey/Soaring peaks.

**Nighttime:**
- TDL: **Electrical Parade Dreamlights** (nightly, still running; its rainy-day substitute Nightfall Glow retired Mar 2026 — rain = cancellation, no backup) + fireworks.
- TDS: **Believe! Sea of Dreams** harbor spectacular (Peter Pan/Moana/Rapunzel/Elsa — perfect for these kids). Stake harbor spots 60–90 min ahead or buy DPA viewing (¥2,500). FLAG: verify Believe! still running / what replaced anniversary-era "Sparkling Jubilee" entertainment in spring 2027.

**Characters:** Tokyo does structured greeting locations (app lists them + wait times); Duffy & Friends are TDS-exclusive and a genuine phenomenon — the 10yo will want a Duffy/LinaBell plush; village-style greeting at Village Greeting Place (ShellieMay). Lines 30–60 min; do one or two max. Free-roaming characters around World Bazaar/American Waterfront at open are the hack.

**Snacks/popcorn culture:** popcorn flavors are a collect-them-all game — flavors rotate by cart location (soy sauce butter, curry, garlic shrimp, matcha-white choc, strawberry cheesecake, honey); refillable themed buckets ¥2,500–4,000. Must-eats: **Little Green Men mochi (Tokyo Disneyland Ice cream cones/TDS)**, **Gyoza Dog (Mysterious Island, TDS — the icon)**, Ukiwa Bun (shrimp, TDS Port Discovery), teriyaki chicken leg, flavored churros, Mike Melon Bread. Kid quest fodder (see below).

**Merch:** prices well below US parks; TDS-exclusive Duffy line + Fantasy Springs merch are the targets. Bon Voyage megastore at Maihama Station for last-chance. Some hot items gated by Standby Pass on app.

**Community wisdom:**
- #1 mistake: underestimating TDS's size — plan geographically, don't criss-cross.
- #2: not having the app + accounts + payment card set up before day 1 (DPA races are lost in minutes).
- #3: skipping shows — Tokyo's shows (Big Band Beat at TDS — jazz, 13yo-friendly) out-class US parks; Big Band Beat may need app lottery (Entry Request) — check.
- Must-not-skip: Journey to the Center of the Earth (TDS's signature coaster-drop dark ride), Soaring Fantastic Flight (superior queue/preshow vs US), Tower of Terror (unique "Hightower" storyline), Pooh's Hunny Hunt (trackless, best-in-class), Enchanted Tale of Beauty and the Beast, Sindbad's Storybook Voyage (sleeper hit, walk-on).
- Overrated/skippable: Indiana Jones if lines spike (Disneyland CA clone), Toy Story Mania (US clone — only if short), 20,000 Leagues walk-through queue times, Monsters Inc Ride & Go Seek (fine but clone-adjacent).
- Bring: passport-photo-free entry is fine, but carry park tickets in app screenshots (cell service in crowds is spotty); portable battery MANDATORY (app use drains phones).

## LOGISTICS

- **Shanghai→Tokyo (May 30):** ~3 hr flight. **Choose HND (Haneda) over NRT** if fares are close — ANA (28x/wk), JAL (21x/wk), Shanghai Airlines serve PVG/SHA→HND; Haneda is 30–40 min from both Maihama AND central Tokyo vs Narita's 60–90 min. SHA (Hongqiao)→HND on JAL/ANA is the premium-convenience pairing.
- **HND→Maihama hotel:** **Airport Limousine Bus direct to Tokyo Disney Resort hotels — ¥1,000 adult/¥500 child, 30–40 min, every ~10 min in mornings, last bus ~7 PM** (train backup: Keikyu→JR Keiyo via Tokyo Stn, ~60–75 min, ¥800ish; taxi ¥12,000–18,000). If landing after 7 PM, train or taxi.
- **IC card:** iPhone users — add **Suica in Apple Wallet** (Settings→Wallet→Transit Card; works with foreign Visa/MC/Amex, no Japanese Apple ID) or the **Welcome Suica Mobile app** (English, tourist-oriented, expires 180 days). One card per person incl. kids (child-fare physical Welcome Suica for under-12s from JR East counters — the 10yo rides half-fare; mobile Suica is adult-fare only, so get the 10yo a physical child card at HND JR East counter. FLAG verify current child-card issuance). Works on all trains/subways/buses/konbini/vending machines nationwide incl. Kyoto.
- **JR Pass verdict: NO.** Itinerary = local Tokyo transit (+Suica) + one-way Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen (~¥14,000/adult w/ reserved seat). 7-day JR Pass (~¥50,000 post-2023-price-hike) is wildly unprofitable. Buy the Shinkansen leg à la carte via **SmartEX app** (English, foreign cards, ticketless w/ Suica integration).
- **Tokyo→Kyoto, Jun 4:** **Nozomi**, Tokyo Stn → Kyoto Stn, ~2h15m, every ~10 min, reserved seats (book the E-seat side, right side facing Kyoto, for Mt. Fuji views ~40 min in — FLAG: D/E side = Fuji side is the *right* side heading west; confirm seat map at booking). **Oversized baggage rule: any bag with L+W+D > 160 cm (most large check-in suitcases) REQUIRES the "seat with oversized baggage area" reservation (free, but limited — book early via SmartEX); ¥1,000 penalty + forced move without it.** Family of 4 with big luggage: reserve the last-row oversized-baggage seats in a car, or ship 1–2 big bags Tokyo→Kyoto hotel via **takkyubin luggage forwarding (Yamato, ~¥2,000–2,500/bag, next-day)** and carry overnight bags — this is the pro move with kids.
- **Navigating with kids:** Google Maps is excellent for Tokyo transit (platform numbers, car positions); avoid rush 7:30–9:00 AM on Yamanote/Chuo; kids under 6 free, 6–11 half fare; station melodies + stamp rally (eki stamps) keep the 10yo engaged.
- **Weather (May 30–Jun 4):** pre-tsuyu window — Kanto rainy season onset averages **~June 7** (varies: May 22 in 2025, June 21 in 2024). Expect highs ~26°C/79°F, lows ~18°C/65°F, humid, 30–50% chance of rain days. Pack: compact umbrellas (or buy ¥600 konbini clear umbrellas — an experience itself), light rain shells, quick-dry shoes. Rain at Disney = shorter lines but Dreamlights cancels (no backup parade since Mar 2026).
- **Etiquette:** trains = quiet, no phone calls, no eating on local trains (Shinkansen eating = encouraged, hence ekiben); stand left on Tokyo escalators; shoes off where indicated (some restaurants, temples); almost no public trash cans — carry a bag for trash (konbini bins are for konbini purchases); **no tipping anywhere, ever** — it can cause genuine confusion; cash still useful (¥10,000–20,000 buffer) though cards/IC now near-universal.
