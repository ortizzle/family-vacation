## LOGISTICS

- **Changi → Marina Bay:** Grab/taxi ~20 min, roughly S$25–45 with evening/airport surcharges ([airporttransfer.com](https://airporttransfer.com/blog/singapore-changi-airport-to-marina-bay-sands), [singapore-spirit](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/changi-airport-to-city/)); MRT ~50 min, ~S$2/pp. **Verdict: Grab on arrival night** (4 people + luggage + jet lag ≈ same cost as 4 MRT fares in sanity terms).
- **Getting around:** everything on this itinerary is walkable or one Grab hop. Ride the MRT once for the experience/kid quest (tap any contactless Visa/Mastercard or Apple/Google Pay directly at the gates — SimplyGo, no card purchase needed; each rider needs their own card/device) ([SimplyGo FAQ](https://simplygo.com.sg/faqs/cards-and-charms/simplygo/contactless-bank-cards/), [SMRT visitor page](https://www.smrt.com.sg/public-transport/getting-around/visiting-singapore/how-to-pay/)).
- **Cruise transfer May 20:** Marina Bay Cruise Centre is ~2 miles from the Marina Bay hotel belt — a 5–10 min taxi/Grab ([rome2rio](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Marina-Bay-Sands/Marina-Bay-Cruise-Centre-Singapore)); walking with luggage not advised in the heat. Leave ~30 min before their assigned DCL port-arrival window; being a public holiday, roads should be calm.
- **Payment:** contactless card/phone works nearly everywhere including transit; **carry ~S$50–100 cash for hawker stalls** (many older stalls are cash-preferred). No tipping — service charge is built into restaurant bills ([Homejourney etiquette guide](https://www.homejourney.sg/blog/singapore-etiquette-guide-cultural-dos-donts-homejourney-202602241601)).
- **Weather mid-May:** inter-monsoon — avg high 32°C/90°F, ~82% humidity, thunderstorms most likely early-to-mid afternoon; May is one of the peak thunderstorm months ([weather.gov.sg](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-climate-of-singapore/), [currentresults](https://www.currentresults.com/Weather/Singapore/weather-in-may.php)). Strategy: outdoors before 11am and after 5pm; domes/malls 12–4pm; rain passes fast — shelter, don't cancel.
- **Etiquette essentials:** no eating/drinking on MRT (S$500 fine); return your own hawker tray (required); "chope" = tissue packet on table means taken; queue culture is serious; escalators stand-left in practice ([travelfish hawker etiquette](https://www.travelfish.org/eatandmeet_profile/singapore/central_region/central_area/downtown_singapore/2294)).
